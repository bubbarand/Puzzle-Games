# Spec 002: Rules of the 20 games

Status: draft. Isaac will add and change rules. Keep rules small; each game must be playable in 2 to 10 minutes.
Skill tags: N numbers, R risk vs return, C choose under constraints, K use a set of capabilities, W advantage in weakness.

## Space
- `traffic-jam` [C]: 6x6 grid, cars (2 or 3 long) slide only along their own axis. Free the red car to the exit. Klotski boards are extra levels.
- `golf-tee` [N,C]: peg solitaire. Jump a peg over a neighbor into an empty hole and remove the jumped peg. Goal: one peg left. Other board shapes (triangle, cross, star) are extra levels.
- `build-square` [C]: some shapes already placed. Drag the given shapes to fill a square (levels may use a pyramid outline). Shapes may rotate.
- `sokoban` [C,W]: push (never pull) crates onto targets. Redesign idea: a crate becomes a different kind of object after each push; keep the classic rule for level 1 to 3.
- `pentomino-5x5`: a 5x5 square (25 cells = 5 pentominoes). Some pentominoes are already fixed. Place the rest to fill the square exactly.

## Quantity
- `bag-draw` [R,K]: you own tiles with values. Before each round you may add or swap tiles in the bag. The round draws N tiles at random (seeded from the level number). Reach the target sum. Limited tries (`maxTries` = 3). Luck is kept. Called a skill game.
- `knapsack` [N,R,C]: items with weight and value, a weight limit. Reach a target total value. Redesign idea: a balloon that rises with value and sinks with weight.
- `resource-allocation` [N,R]: 10 coins, 4 to 6 options (levels 1-2 have 4, levels 3-4 have 5, level 5 has 6; each level has one winning split). Each option shows a fixed 5-year return table and a risk number. Reach a target total return while the worst year stays above a limit. No luck (all years shown).
- `balance-scale` [N,C]: shapes have hidden integer weights. Scales show equalities. Find the weight of each shape, then balance a final scale.
- `shape-sudoku` [C]: 4x4 or 6x6 Latin square using shapes instead of numbers. Fill the grid so no shape repeats in a row or column. Some cells are given.

## Direction
- `mirror-laser` [C,K]: a laser enters a grid. Place or rotate a limited number of mirrors so the beam reaches the target (and passes through required checkpoints). Limited tries (`maxTries` = 5 submits; moving mirrors before submit is free).
- `monument-path` [C]: a small traveler walks along a path across blocks. Rotating the whole level in 90 degree steps connects paths that look broken from other angles. Our own level design, not a copy.
- `network-flow` [N,C]: pipes with capacities connect a source to sinks. Route the flow to meet every sink's demand.
- `lemmings-tools` [K,C]: little creatures walk forward on a path. You have a limited set of tools (blocker, bridge, turn arrow, digger). Place tools on tiles so at least N creatures reach the exit. Turn-based: you place tools, then press run (a deterministic simulation).

## Sequence
- `breach-turns` [K,W,C]: our own version of Into the Breach. 6x6 board, 3 units, enemies show their next attack. Each turn you move and act with each unit. Goal: survive N turns with at least M buildings. Pushing enemies into each other is the main trick. Deterministic.
- `lights-out` [C]: 5x5 grid of lights. Pressing a cell flips it and its four neighbors. Turn all lights off. Levels are generated by random presses from solved so they are solvable.
- `hanoi` [C]: 3 to 5 pegs, 3 to 7 discs. Move one disc at a time, never a bigger disc on a smaller one. Best score = known minimum.
- `tower-fall` [C,K]: tower-defense with the waves known in advance (shown as a timeline). You place a limited number of towers on a path before the run; a deterministic simulation runs; survive all waves.
- `matrix-reasoning` [C]: 3x3 grid of shapes follows row and column rules (count, rotation, fill). Pick the missing cell from 6 options. Level data is generated from a rule list.
- `domino-logic` [N]: a set of dominoes follows a hidden rule (sum, difference, or sequence). Fill the empty half of the last domino.
