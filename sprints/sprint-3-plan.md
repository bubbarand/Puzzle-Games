# Sprint 3 Plan

**Goal:** 30 puzzles, playable, and a working, beautiful UI.

**Why this:** This is the right thing to build because it's a problem that I want to have solved in my week. Truly making something that solves a problem for me is the most important thing that I could be doing because problem-solving is what makes a great product.

**Done looks like:** I can play each day without it breaking, I have invited one other person and they have joined the app.

**Predicted difficulty:** 5

## Brainstorm (how the 30-puzzle goal was shaped)

1. **Ideas gathered:** several rounds of puzzle ideas (classic, physical, Coolmath-style, "randomness" games) were collected in the Notion "Puzzle Ideas" page. Each idea was checked against the product rules: no words, visual, one player, free undo (laser and bag-draw have limited tries), and a star rating against a best score.
2. **Result:** 20 puzzle types in 4 classes, each with its own look. Space (cartoon, warm), Quantity (sleek, cool), Direction (isometric 3D), Sequence (jelly on blueprint). Each type has 5 levels, so 100 puzzles in total.
3. **Progression:** finish all puzzles in a day to earn a new type. Miss a day and the newest type is removed (it can be earned back first). 7 earning days in a row give one freeze that protects a missed day. Rules: `specs/004-daily-progression.md`.
4. **Specs and design:** `specs/001` (game logic), `002` (game rules), `003` (game screen), `004` (progression); design checklist in `design/`.
5. **Not chosen yet:** more ideas from the Coolmath, classic and "randomness" lists. They can add types after the first invited player joins.
